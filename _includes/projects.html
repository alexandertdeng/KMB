Monitoring

VENTO

The world’s smallest portable, non-invasive CO₂ wearable, built by Keep Me Breathing. VENTO reads the one signal that matters most to a child with CCHS, carbon dioxide, continuously, comfortably and without needles or hospital hardware.

Patent pending

Next stage

A closed-loop system

Building on VENTO’s continuous CO₂ readings, a closed-loop system would sense what the body needs and adjust breathing support automatically, rather than relying on fixed ventilator settings.
