Treatment

The Breathing Pacemaker

A fully implantable biofeedback phrenic-nerve pacing system, the world’s first treatment designed specifically for CCHS. Like a cardiac pacemaker for breathing, it will sense what the body needs and adjust the rate and depth of every breath automatically. No wires, no external kit, no mechanical ventilator.

First patent filed

Monitoring

VENTO

The world’s smallest portable, non-invasive CO₂ wearable, built by Keep Me Breathing. VENTO reads the one signal that matters most to a child with CCHS, carbon dioxide, continuously, comfortably and without needles or hospital hardware.
