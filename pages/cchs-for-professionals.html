Congenital Central Hypoventilation Syndrome (CCHS) is a neurocristopathy characterized by hypoventilation and ANS dysregulation

What causes CCHS?

Congenital Central Hypoventilation Syndrome is caused by mutations in the paired-like homeobox gene PHOX2B, which alters control of breathing and autonomic nervous system regulation, necessitating artificial ventilation as life-support.

Our Plan and Roadmap

The effects of being born with CCHS are devastating, with no cure and limited therapeutic options available to patients while they sleep beyond artificial life support. We are addressing the urgent need for advanced treatments that can both improve and save the lives of people diagnosed with CCHS by combining advances in innovation and technology with the known science of CCHS and a relentless approach to getting results.

To face down that challenge we have built a world class team within our Medical and Entrepreneur Advisory Board. Our immediate project action will be building an advanced Phrenic Nerve Pacer that is truly implantable and delivers on our promise to make a world of difference to patients, and save lives.
