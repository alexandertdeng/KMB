Here are a few tips to help get you started

  1. 01Tell us about your activity or challenge

    Please let us know any details of your fundraising event or challenge a month in advance ideally. We will add the event date and details onto our website.

  2. 02Event planner

    Use our event planning template to help you plan your event or activity to make sure you have everything covered and enough people to help you run it!

    Download your event planner

  3. 03Set up a fundraising page

    If you don’t have an account with JustGiving it’s super easy to set it up by registering with your email and choose Keep Me Breathing as your chosen charity. We are already registered with them so our bank details are already linked to this site, which means all money raised will be automatically transferred to the charity. You can set up an account here: www.justgiving.com.

    1. Select the button: fundraising for a charity (Keep Me Breathing)
    2. Add details of your event or challenge
    3. Set your fundraising target
    4. Add our charity logo. Download our logo here: Logo
    5. Add an optional paragraph about Keep Me Breathing to your page.

    You can of course use alternative crowdfunding sites such as GoFundMe or Fundly, just make sure our charity is linked.

  4. 04Sponsorship form

    If your event includes gathering sponsorship from friends and family here is a template form you can use to add your own details: Sponsorship Form Resource

    Ask your employer, school or college if they would consider match funding what you raise!

  5. 05Social media posts

    Create your own social media posts to announce the challenge or event and keep posting on your own accounts/ across other local facebook groups in your area and encourage friends and family to share.

    Here are some template social media posts that you may find helpful. Just add your own event details.

    Download template

    Please tag us in any social posts (Facebook is @keepmebreathingfb and Instagram is @keepmebreathinginsta). This means we can share any social posts you create across our own charity channels to boost awareness and donations.

    Continue posting ideally at least a month in advance and upload any photos from the challenge / event during and afterwards to maximise the amount of potential donations.

    Thank people for their support and post how much money you raised. We will also post how much money was raised for the charity and of course a big thank you for your support.

    Tip: If it’s a big enough or unique challenge then local press may be interested in hearing about it, so consider contacting your local paper or radio station and email them the details. They may be interested in sending a reporter to interview you/ the team.

  6. 06Posters or flyers

    Depending on what your event is, you may also be able to put up posters or hand out flyers in your local community. Please be aware we can’t unfortunately cover any printing costs for individual events or challenges. We can provide you with a high resolution version of our logo for any printing. Download our logo here: Logo

  7. 07Merchandise & charity branding

    We can provide branded collection tins and branded T-shirts for events and also have a pull-up banner that can be borrowed. Sadly, we can’t provide T-shirts for free but they can be purchased from our shop, or contact {{ site.email.sarah }} to discuss options.

  8. 08Let us know your results and how much you raised

    We naturally want to feature stories of our fundraisers in our newsletter/ on our website and across our social channels so please keep us updated on your final achievements and provide us with any photos, so we can shout about it.

Skydive for Keep Me Breathing

Take a breathtaking leap for CCHS!

If you’re ready to make a real difference whilst experiencing the thrill of a lifetime, a skydive can be a fantastic way to raise funds to support us!

Whether it’s your first skydive or you’re a seasoned adrenaline seeker, taking a courageous leap from the skies will help us to keep doing the work that we are doing.

Skydiving gives an exhilarating rush of freedom. As you step from the plane there’s a thrilling moment of suspense, before plunging into a breathtaking freefall and then, when the parachute opens, a serene drift follows, filling you with an awe and exhilaration that you’ll always remember.

You can arrange a fundraising skydive for Keep Me Breathing in many stunning locations across the UK by following the link.

  • Variety of dates available across different venues
  • Reg fee £70

Minimum fundraising amount £395 – £450 depending on airfield and cost of the jump

The registration fee is all you need to pay if you achieve the minimum target amount. If you have not reached your target two weeks before the jump date you will be asked to pay for the jump which ranges from £190-£250 depending on the airfield.

Before you register, please ensure you have read and fully understood the challenge terms and conditions.
