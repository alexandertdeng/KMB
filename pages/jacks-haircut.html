Jack’s Hair-Raising Challenge

It’s official: Jack is taking a deep breath as he’s about to wave goodbye to his beloved dreadlocks and get the dreaded chop – all for Keep Me Breathing!

Jack’s long dreadlocks have been his pride and joy, in fact he has been growing and tending to them for more than 6 years, but now they’re going under the scissors to raise funds to support our ongoing vital research and work.

But here’s the twist—YOU get to choose Jack’s new look!

The hairstyle that receives the most votes wins. You’ll be able to choose between:

Option 1: The Mullet – Business at the front, party at the back – because why not?

Option 2: The Fade – Classic short back and sides, clean and sophisticated.

Option 3: The Mohican – Bold, daring, and a little wild.
