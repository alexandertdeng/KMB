JOGLE + 3 PEAKS
A REMARKABLE CHALLENGE

Over 1,000 miles!

Nikki Love, long-distance runner, will run from John O’Groats to Land’s End via the 3 peaks of Ben Nevis, Scafell Pike and Snowdon (Yr Wyddfa) – and set the Women’s Fastest Known Time along the way!

The route is just over 1,000 miles and Nikki aims to complete it in 30 days or less, running approximately 30-35 miles per day. The start will take place on Tuesday 9 July at John O’Groats with a proposed finish date of 8 August at Land’s End.

Support Nikki and Keep Me Breathing

Introducing Nikki

Nikki Love is an adventure runner, author, speaker, and a motivator – dreaming big, taking action and achieving extraordinary goals. In 2023, Nikki became the fastest women to run across Australia from Perth to Sydney – 4,044kms (2512 miles) in 76 days, 3 hours and 43 minutes. She achieved this goal off the back of the previous “training runs” she’s undertaken including: Running 63 marathons in 63 days through the UK, a craic of a 750 mile run around Ireland, a runcation through Malta and around Gozo, a jolly jaunt across the Netherlands, an extremely muddy and cow-pat laden run from top to bottom of Wales.

Driven by her curiosity to answer the question “I wonder if I can do that…” Nikki decided it was time to take her love for travel, marathon running, and cross-country running to a new level.

A RACE WHICH IS SUPPORTING OUR RACE!

Nikki’s challenge will be raising money for Keep Me Breathing, to support our product development work and helping us to take our work to the next stage. We have families around the world counting on us to bring a new solution to patients with CCHS, replacing the need for a ventilator and transforming lives.

You could say both Nikki and Keep Me Breathing are in a race! Our race is to raise sufficient funds as quickly as we can so we can complete our 5-year product-to-patient plan, designed by Cambridge University. This means we will reach our target of bringing this technical innovation to families within 5 years.
