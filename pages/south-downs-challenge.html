The South Downs Challenge promises to be an adventure that will keep you breathing all the way to the finish line!

Beginning just outside of Brighton, this 42km trek will take you along a stunning, remote section of the beautiful South Downs National Trail and is set against the dramatic backdrop of the South Downs National Park. The pathway descends downwards to the picturesque village of Alfriston, where you’ll follow the meandering Cuckmere river out to the sea before trekking along the cliff tops of the Seven Sisters to the finish.

This is a fully supported trekking challenge led by Charity Challenge events, where you’ll be participating in a fun group alongside others.

  • 9 August 2025
  • Reg fee £45

Minimum fundraising amount £235

At least 80% of the minimum sponsorship required (£188.00) must be sent to Keep Me Breathing 11 weeks before departure (by 24/05/2025), and the remaining 20% (£47.00) within 4 weeks of completing the challenge (by 06/09/2025).

Before you register, please ensure you have read and fully understood the challenge events terms and conditions.
