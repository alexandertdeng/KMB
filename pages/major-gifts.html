Has your child just been diagnosed? First, breathe. Our guide for newly diagnosed families covers what CCHS means and what to do first. James and Steph have been exactly where you are, and are happy to talk whether or not you are thinking about giving.

Why a major gift matters

No one else is going to build this

For children like Casper, Beau and Tiger-Lily, and thousands of others around the world, the standard treatment is to be connected to artificial life support for the rest of their lives.

Because the “market size” for CCHS is so small, no pharmaceutical or med tech company has any plans to invest in CCHS. So it’s down to us, and people like you.

Real progressA patent filed for continuous CO₂ monitoring, and VENTO, our wearable CO₂ monitor, validated by the Extreme Environments Lab at the University of Portsmouth.
Backed by scienceOur accelerated five-year plan is endorsed by over 130 doctors, scientists and medical professionals.
Lean by designA small core team means over 90p of every £1 goes straight into developing treatment.
Run like a start-upFounded by Casper’s parents and guided by a medical and entrepreneurial advisory board of CCHS specialists and founders.
