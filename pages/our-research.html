What we’re building

Two programmes, one goal

Every child with CCHS sleeping safely, without a ventilator.

{% include projects.html anchors=true %}
  • Active research projects
  • Global collaborations
  • Drug development research
  • Drug repurposing research
  • A truly implantable biofeedback diaphragm pacer system, removing the need for mechanical ventilation
  • New technology capable of automatically adjusting breathing rate and tidal volume during sleep

Our plan

Plan and roadmap

We are addressing the urgent need for advanced treatments that can both improve and save the lives of people diagnosed with CCHS, by combining advances in innovation and technology with the known science of CCHS and a relentless approach to getting results.

To face down that challenge we have built a world class team within our Medical and Entrepreneurial Advisory Board.

Meet our advisory board

Collaboration

Keep Me Breathing × Cambridge University

We’re excited to be working with The Cambridge University Consulting Network to research and move forward with an advanced diaphragm pacer.

The consulting network has a strong history of working in the rare disease field, and we’ll keep you fully updated on progress as it is made.
