Why we exist

Founded for children and families living with CCHS

Keep Me Breathing was founded to develop treatments for children and families affected by Congenital Central Hypoventilation Syndrome (CCHS), a rare condition that causes patients to stop breathing every time they go to sleep, and often during simple daily activities such as watching television or even when they have a common cold.

CCHS has always been and will always be at the heart of our mission, and is the reason we’re developing VENTO: a lightweight, non-invasive and wearable technology designed to monitor carbon dioxide continuously during sleep, activity and everyday life. VENTO forms the foundation of a closed-loop system further down the line.

VENTO and our research

Beyond CCHS

Technology that could help far more people

Serendipitously, through developing VENTO, we’ve recognised that this technology could also have an impact far beyond CCHS. Inadequate breathing and rising carbon dioxide also affect children and adults living with a range of respiratory, neuromuscular and sleep-related conditions.

Subject to successful validation and regulatory approval, VENTO could potentially support selected patients with conditions including:

  • Sleep apnoea
  • Motor neurone disease
  • Muscular dystrophy
  • Obesity hypoventilation syndrome
  • COPD
  • Spinal cord injury
  • Other causes of chronic respiratory failure

Genetic testing

Recognising CCHS in unexplained infant and child deaths

Keep Me Breathing has also lobbied the NHS Genomic Medicine Service for inclusion of the PHOX2B gene in the genetic testing for ‘Unexpected death in infancy and sudden unexplained death in childhood’.

This request is now under technical review with expert scientific teams and we are optimistic for its eventual inclusion as an important genetic cause of sudden infant and childhood death.
