The Team

To urgently address the challenge of developing an advanced treatment for a condition where artificial life support is the norm, we are proud to have an exceptional scientific and entrepreneurial advisory board to guide us throughout the process

Our Medical and Entrepreneurial Advisory Board

Dr Melita Irving

Dr Melita Irving is an expert London-based consultant and honorary reader in clinical genetics. She specialises in genetic conditions, and acts as the chief investigator for clinical trials in children with skeletal dysplasia. Her work is featured in many peer-reviewed publications and she has published extensively on various aspects of clinical genetics.

Dr Alexander Deng

Dr Alexander T Deng is a Clinical Geneticist. He is Lead for Computer Vision & Medical Imaging: Clinical AI Interest Group at The Alan Turing Institute. An AI Fellow at Guy's and St Thomas' Hospital, Dr Deng received the Yamanouchi Cell Biology Prize from Oxford University

Dr Nick Sireau

Nick Sireau, PhD, is an entrepreneur and the CEO and Chair of Trustees at the AKU Society, and co-founder and Chair of Beacon, an organisation that helps all rare disease patient groups. He is the editor of ‘Rare Diseases: Challenges and Opportunities for Social Entrepreneurs’ (Greenleaf 2013) and of the 'Patient Group Handbook: A Practical Guide for Research and Drug Development' (Findacure 2016).

Lara Morgan

Lara is a British entrepreneur with a track record of delivering exceptional accelerated growth. She is an inspirational leader with a legacy of building world-class teams and aligning organisations behind a clear strategy. She authored the book; ‘More balls than most’ and is now involved in several UK startups.

Prof Debra Weese-Mayer

Debra Weese-Mayer is a leading authority for CCHS internationally and passionate advocate for children with the disease. She is a Professor in Pediatric Autonomic Medicine at Northwestern University and Chief of the first Center for Autonomic Medicine in Pediatrics (CAMP) at Lurie Children’s Hospital in Chicago. She patented a genetic test to diagnose CCHS, allowing early confirmation of diagnosis, and prenatal testing (patent proceeds support CCHS research).

Xenia Proton De La Chapelle

Xenia is a biotech entrepreneur and held management positions in several large international companies before founding a textiles company. Closely affected by CCHS, Xenia took over the presidency of the French CCHS Family Association in 2017 before founding AtmosR. Xenia is also a member of the Strategic Advisory Board of the MarMaRa Institute (Marseille Institute for Rare Diseases).

Casey Rand

Casey Rand is the Clinical Research and Project Manager at Lurie Children's Hospital and Stanley Manne Research Institute with training in genetics and data science. He has nearly 2 decades of experience investigating the cause and consequences of CCHS, authoring over 25 research papers and several chapters focused on CCHS. He is a member of the world’s leading specialist care center for CCHS, The Center for Autonomic Medicine in Pediatrics (CAMP).

Dr Nathan Beckouche

Nathan has worked in drug research and development for over 10 years. Holder of a PhD in biology, physiology and physiopathology (UPMC, Paris), he worked in translational research at UPMC and at the University of San Francisco, California, before joining Alexion Pharmaceuticals as preclinical project manager and now directly researches CCHS at AtmosR.

Jason Burbach

We are delighted to announce Jason Burbach has agreed to join our Advisory Board as of June 2024. Jason completed both his undergraduate BSC and his Masters of Science, both in Aerospace Engineering. Early in his career he designed, built and tested guided missile systems before focusing on interplanetary space exploration. He was the lead ground software/simulation engineer for over half a dozen separate NASA missions to the Moon, Mars, Jupiter, and the asteroid Bennu. Currently he is the Lead Software Architect and Designer for a team of spacecraft engineers that oversees a constellation of earth imaging satellites. Jason's son Tristan was diagnosed at birth with CCHS. Jason serves on the CCHS Network Research Advisory Board as an industry research associate and statistical data scientist."

Amanda Smit

Amanda is the Chair of CCHS UK, the family support network for parents, families and patients directly affected by CCHS. Amanda has a beautiful daughter aged 2, Georgia, who has CCHS.

Anita Kaushal

Anita Kaushal is a businesswoman, entrepreneur, Ayurvedic expert, fundraiser and creative consultant. All Anita’s projects share a strong vision and a common thread – to enrich the everyday – physically, mentally and emotionally. A life-changing incident lead Anita to reevaluate where she wished to take her business/ She founded Mauli. Available through some of the world’s most exclusive retail and spas including Net-A-Porter and Bulgari.

Dr Reshma Amin

Dr Reshma Amin is a paediatric respirologist at the Hospital for Sick Children (SickKids) in Toronto, where she is director of the sleep medicine and home ventilation programmes. Her work spans clinical care and clinical research, and she follows children with CCHS. She advises Keep Me Breathing on the clinical development of VENTO.

Team & Partners

Stephanie Roberts - Founder

Casper & Max's Mum. This is not a choice. The technology to make a world of difference to the lives of children with CCHS is there. But the investment is not. To be independent. To go to school. To go for a sleepover at a friends house. To sleep without artificial life support. We will change that, for everyone with CCHS now, and everyone born with CCHS in the future.

Casper & Max's Dad. On 1st December 2021 I was a 32 year old business owner with an amazing partner and our first child, travelling the world. That Charity is called Keep Me Breathing.
