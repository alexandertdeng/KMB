Registered charity no. 1201757 | Brighton, UK

Driving cures and treatments for CCHS

Children born with Congenital Central Hypoventilation Syndrome (CCHS) stop breathing when they fall asleep. There is no cure and no treatment beyond artificial ventilation, yet. Keep Me Breathing exists to change that, by researching cures and developing and funding advanced technology that will let every child with CCHS sleep safely.
