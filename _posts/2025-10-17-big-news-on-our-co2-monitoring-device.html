As of this week, we can officially announce that Keep Me Breathing has filed its first patent for technology to begin making a real difference to children born with CCHS. This patent is for providing CO2 monitoring in a non-invasive way, via our smart wearable CO2 monitor. This monitor will not only make a difference now by alerting patients and families when CO2 is too high, but will be able to “talk” with our Breathing Pacemaker once it is developed, to allow the device to help patients breathe more deeply when they need to, for example during exercise or illness. Please meet…VENTO! VENTO is over 90% lighter and 20 times smaller than the equivalent medical grade equipment currently used to monitor CCHS patients’ CO2 levels, and represents the completion of stage one of our three stage plan to create our advanced Breathing Pacemaker.
Having already passed more than 100 initial feasibility tests, we’re now pushing ahead with further testing and calling on support to help us – so if you know of anyone who could become an early partner or help us – please do get in touch.

VENTO has also gained promising early interest in the sports and athletics arena and so, with this wider market appeal – every VENTO sold in the future will directly feed into our funding and work.

THANK YOU again for your continued support and helping us to achieve this critical milestone.
