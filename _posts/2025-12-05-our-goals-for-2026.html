Here is some of what we’ve achieved with… image: /assets/uploads/2025/12/Sarah-KMB-work-10-scaled.jpg wp_id: 7754 --- In 2025, KMB has invested over £175,000 into CCHS treatment research and development as a result of your support. Thank you. Here is some of what we’ve achieved with the time and money you’ve so kindly given:
  • Built VENTO, the world’s smallest portable, non invasive CO2 wearable
  • Filed the patent for VENTO
  • Begun work on stage 2 of our 5 year roadmap
Thanks to you, we are on track to meet our goal to build our advanced breathing pacemaker for CCHS patients. And we’re not stopping. Whatever it takes to bring this breathing pacemaker to CCHS children as quickly as we can, that is what we are doing. We’re aiming for 2026 to be our biggest year yet in progressing our advanced breathing pacemaker. Over the next 12 months, our goal is to invest £250,000 to: 1) Trial VENTO – our patent-pending CO2 monitor – for use with CCHS patients 2) Bring VENTO to commercial release 3) Continue forward with the remaining phases or our advanced breathing pacemaker 5 year road map Important to know: Every VENTO sold commercially to athletes and sports enthusiasts will directly fund our charity’s work, helping us undertake the final phases of our 5 year roadmap – including deep clinical testing of the breathing pacemaker. From next year, you’ll start to see VENTO available commercially, expanding our fundraising potential and accelerating the path to delivering this life-changing technology for CCHS children. Your help spreading the word to friends, family, investors, and partners will make all the difference. We’re incredibly proud to remain a high-impact, lean charity. In year one, 100% of donations went directly into developing CCHS treatments and, nearly three years on – with a small (but mighty!) core team of three – over 90% of every pound donated still goes straight into building this technology. Every donation, gift, grant and fundraiser matters. We never forget the time, care and trust behind each one – and we always focus on return on investment for children with CCHS. We continue to put every penny possible directly into the treatments we are developing to bring CCHS families safety, relief and freedom. And we promise we always will. On behalf of all of us at Keep Me Breathing, and the CCHS patients and families we serve, thank you for your loyalty, belief, and support. You make this mission possible. 2026 is a critical year for us. Please stay close – we’ll keep sharing our progress.
