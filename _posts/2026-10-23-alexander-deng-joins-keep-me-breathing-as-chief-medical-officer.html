We’re delighted to welcome Alexander Deng to Keep Me Breathing as our Chief Medical Officer.

Dr Alexander T Deng is a Clinical Geneticist who already knows Keep Me Breathing well as a member of our Medical and Entrepreneurial Advisory Board. Alex is Lead for Computer Vision & Medical Imaging in the Clinical AI Interest Group at The Alan Turing Institute, an AI Fellow at Guy’s and St Thomas’ Hospital, and received the Yamanouchi Cell Biology Prize from Oxford University.

As Chief Medical Officer, Alex will help guide the clinical side of our work as VENTO moves towards validation with patients. Alex has also been hard at work behind the scenes, rebuilding this website so that families, clinicians and supporters can find what they need more easily, from a newly diagnosed family looking for answers to a philanthropist wanting to fund the next breakthrough.

Welcome to the team, Alex!
