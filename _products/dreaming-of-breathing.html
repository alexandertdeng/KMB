Dreaming of Breathing is a magical tale inspired by two friends, Casper and Beau, who share the same rare breathing condition.

At night as they get ready for bed they must be connected to a ventilator as they cannot breathe by themselves when they sleep.

They plan to meet in their dreams and have wonderful adventures together as they dive for treasure and even take a ride on a whale!
